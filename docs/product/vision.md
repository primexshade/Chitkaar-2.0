# Chitkaar 2.0 — Product Vision

## Purpose

Chitkaar 2.0 is the digital home of Chitkaar Welfare Society, a youth-led
organization working to uplift women, children, rural communities, and
underprivileged communities.

The website should communicate that Chitkaar is:

- Modern
- Trustworthy
- Youth-led
- Human
- Hopeful
- Fearless
- Community-driven

## Mission

Chitkaar's primary mission is to uplift women and children.

Its areas of work include:

- Education
- Healthcare
- Livelihood
- Women empowerment
- Child welfare
- Community development
- Charity and donation initiatives

## Origin

Chitkaar was started by Ishita Jaiswal while she was still in school.

It began with a focus on women upliftment and was started together with
friends. This origin story is central to the organization's identity as a
youth-led movement.

Chitkaar currently has approximately 25 members.

## Audience

The website is intended for everyone, including:

- Potential volunteers
- Donors
- Event participants
- Community members
- Supporters
- People interested in the organization's work
- Potential partners

## Primary User Journey

The desired visitor journey is:

Discover
→ Understand
→ Trust
→ Explore
→ Feel connected
→ Act

## Primary Actions

The website should encourage visitors to:

1. Join Us
2. Donate
3. Attend an event
4. Explore Chitkaar's work
5. Volunteer
6. Contact the organization

## Brand Personality

The visual and verbal identity should feel:

- Youthful
- Self-dependent
- Fearless
- Empowering
- Hopeful
- Warm
- Human
- Credible
- Energetic

## Women Empowerment

Women empowerment is a major focus and the historical foundation of Chitkaar.

The website should give this mission significant visual and narrative
importance without suggesting that Chitkaar works exclusively with women.

Chitkaar works for everyone.

## Authenticity Rules

The website must never fabricate:

- Impact statistics
- Testimonials
- Partnerships
- Certifications
- Awards
- Projects
- Financial information
- People
- Quotes
- Achievements

Only verified information may be presented as fact.

Real Chitkaar photography should be prioritized over stock imagery.

## Future Marketplace

A future marketplace will allow the organization to sell products made by
women from underserved communities, including items such as jute bags,
T-shirts, and other handmade products.

The marketplace represents the intersection of:

Women empowerment
+
Livelihood
+
Economic opportunity

The marketplace architecture should be planned for future integration, but
fake products, prices, inventory, or payment systems must not be created.

## Success Criteria

A first-time visitor should immediately understand:

1. What Chitkaar is.
2. What Chitkaar does.
3. Who Chitkaar helps.
4. Why Chitkaar can be trusted.
5. How they can participate.

The ideal emotional reaction is:

> "This is a modern, trustworthy organization run by people who genuinely
> care and actually take action."

The desired final conversion is:

> "I want to join."
